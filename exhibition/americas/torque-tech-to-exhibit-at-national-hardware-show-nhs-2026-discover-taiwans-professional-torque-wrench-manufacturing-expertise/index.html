Torque-Tech to Exhibit at National Hardware Show (NHS) 2026 – Discover Taiwan’s Professional Torque Wrench Manufacturing Expertise

Mar 06, 2026

Discover Global Hardware Innovation at NHS 2026

The National Hardware Show (NHS) is one of the most influential trade exhibitions in the global hardware and home improvement industry. Held annually in Las Vegas, USA, the event brings together manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and procurement professionals from around the world.

The show focuses on innovative tools, hardware technologies, and sourcing solutions, providing an important platform where buyers can discover reliable manufacturing partners and new product innovations.

In 2026, the National Hardware Show will take place from March 31 to April 2 at the Las Vegas Convention Center, attracting thousands of global buyers seeking high-quality hardware tools and supply chain partners.

For professionals searching for reliable torque wrench manufacturers, OEM/ODM partners, and advanced torque tool solutions, NHS 2026 is a must-attend event.

Precision, Certification, and Quality You Can Trust

For professional tool buyers, product accuracy, testing standards, and quality assurance are critical factors when selecting a torque tool supplier.

Torque-Tech invests heavily in advanced testing and inspection technologies to ensure every torque tool meets strict international standards.

Our in-house testing laboratory, accredited by TAF (Taiwan Accreditation Foundation), is granted the ILAC Laboratory Combined MRA Mark, ensuring globally recognized calibration and testing reliability.

To further strengthen our quality inspection capability, we introduced the KEYENCE VR-6000 series 3D Optical Profilometer in 2023. This advanced measurement technology enhances precision inspection for critical torque components.

In addition, to support our long-term global growth, Torque-Tech plans to expand our manufacturing facility from 6,500㎡ to 13,000㎡, with construction scheduled to begin by the end of 2026, significantly increasing production capacity and service capabilities.
