Key North American Manufacturing Exhibitions to Watch in H2 2026

Jul 17, 2026

For OEM/ODM buyers and industrial procurement teams in North America, the second half of the year concentrates the region's largest manufacturing technology, packaging, and assembly shows into a tight September-to-October window in the Chicago area. Planning which shows to attend, and which supplier meetings to schedule around them, is easier with a consolidated view of what is confirmed for H2 2026.

IMTS 2026: The Anchor Event for Manufacturing Technology

The single largest event on the North American calendar this year is IMTS 2026, running September 14 to 19 at McCormick Place in Chicago. As the largest manufacturing technology trade show in the Western Hemisphere, IMTS draws more than 89,000 registrants and fills over 1.2 million square feet of exhibit space, covering the full manufacturing technology stack from metal cutting and additive manufacturing to automation, robotics, tooling, and digital manufacturing systems.

Because IMTS runs on a biennial cycle, this is the primary opportunity this cycle for buyers to evaluate CNC machining, grinding, and automation equipment suppliers side by side under one roof. Missing this year's edition means waiting until the next cycle for the next North American IMTS event in this format.

PACK EXPO International and the ASSEMBLY Show

October follows with two more significant shows in the Chicago area. PACK EXPO International runs October 18 to 21 at McCormick Place, drawing an estimated 48,000 attendees and more than 2,600 exhibitors across 1.3 million square feet, making it the primary venue for packaging and processing equipment sourcing in the region.

Later that same month, the ASSEMBLY Show runs October 27 to 29 at the Donald E. Stephens Convention Center in Rosemont, Illinois, with roughly 10,000 attendees and 200-plus exhibitors focused specifically on assembly technology, fastening, and joining equipment. For buyers with both packaging and discrete assembly sourcing needs, the two shows sit close enough together on the calendar to combine into a single trip.

The full North American H2 2026 calendar is summarized below.

Show Dates Location Focus
IMTS 2026 Sep 14-19 Chicago, Illinois, USA Manufacturing technology (CNC, additive, automation, robotics)
PACK EXPO International Oct 18-21 Chicago, Illinois, USA Packaging and processing equipment
ASSEMBLY Show Oct 27-29 Rosemont, Illinois, USA Assembly, fastening, joining equipment

How OEM/ODM Buyers Should Prioritize North American Show Attendance

With three major shows concentrated in a six-week window, prioritization comes down to a few practical factors. First, match the show to a specific sourcing need rather than attending broadly: a buyer focused on CNC machining or automation equipment gets far more value from IMTS than from a packaging-focused show, and vice versa. Second, factor in IMTS's biennial cycle specifically, since missing this year's edition means a two-year wait for the next one in this venue and format.

Booking travel and supplier meetings early matters more in 2026 than in past cycles, since the Chicago area is hosting multiple large-scale events in September and October, putting pressure on hotel and meeting room availability well in advance of the show dates. Buyers planning to attend both IMTS and PACK EXPO or the ASSEMBLY Show should book accommodations as soon as their travel plans are set rather than waiting until closer to the show.

FAQ

Q: Which is the largest manufacturing trade show in North America in H2 2026?

A: IMTS 2026, running September 14 to 19 at McCormick Place in Chicago, is the largest, with more than 89,000 registrants and over 1.2 million square feet of exhibit space covering the full manufacturing technology stack. It runs on a biennial cycle, so this year's edition is the only IMTS edition in North America during this two-year cycle.

Q: Can a single trip cover both IMTS and the packaging or assembly shows in October?

A: A single extended trip could cover all three shows, but it would require staying in the Chicago area from mid-September through late October. For most buyers, PACK EXPO International and the ASSEMBLY Show are the practical pair to combine, since they run October 18-21 and October 27-29, respectively, in the same metropolitan area.

Conclusion

The H2 2026 North American exhibition calendar gives OEM/ODM buyers a concentrated set of opportunities to evaluate manufacturing technology, packaging, and assembly equipment suppliers, all within a single region and a roughly six-week window. Because IMTS runs on a biennial cycle, this year's edition carries more weight for buyers planning supplier evaluation and facility visits than a typical annual cycle would. Prioritizing shows by specific sourcing category, and booking Chicago-area travel early given the clustered event schedule, is the most efficient way to make use of this year's calendar.
