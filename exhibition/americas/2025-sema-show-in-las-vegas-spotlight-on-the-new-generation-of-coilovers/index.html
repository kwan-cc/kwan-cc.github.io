2025 SEMA SHOW in Las Vegas - Spotlight on The New Generation of Coilovers

Mar 31, 2026

2025 SEMA SHOW Overview

Date: November 4–7, 2025

  • Location: Las Vegas Convention Center, USA
  • Nature: The world’s most influential aftermarket automotive show, focusing on new product launches, technical demonstrations, and media exposure.

Focus: New Developments in Coilover Suspension

At SEMA 2025, many major brands will showcase next-generation coilover systems and related shock absorbers, with attention on:

  • Electronic damping (multiple-speed or multi-mode adjustability)
  • Lightweight materials & improved corrosion resistance
  • Hybrid suspension solutions (coilover + air assist etc.)
  • Digital integration — sensor data, app control, etc.

These advances are especially important for manufacturers, dealers, and media wanting to see where the industry is headed.

Key Exhibitors Confirmed & Their Coilover/Releated Involvements

Here are some relevant brands confirmed as participating manufacturers for SEMA 2025, along with their coilover / suspension-related developments:

Exhibitor Participation Coilover / Suspension Relevance
Air Lift Company On the 2025 participating manufacturer list. Also through its brand Air Lift Performance which has recently introduced a Coilover Suspension Kit product line. Their coilover kit (P/N 79002) shows features such as 32-way damping adjustment, threaded body dampers, height adjustability, etc. That’s directly in line with “next-gen coilovers.”
KW Automotive North America KW is also listed among the participating manufacturers for SEMA 2025. There are recent press/releases about KW’s involvement in high-performance coilover systems, including the ST Suspension XTA plus 3 coilovers used by driver Ryan Tuerck. KW is strongly associated with premium coilover suspension kits; their upcoming exposure at SEMA will likely include their latest variants, adjustability, etc.
YELLOW SPEED RACING YELLOWSPEED has officially announced its participation in LAS VEGAS SEMA SHOW 2025, Booth No. 21209, Date: November 4-7, 2025. Yellow Speed Their product page for Dynamic Pro Sport coilovers offers details on their latest coilover line. Their Dynamic Pro Sport coilovers feature 33-way damper adjustability, full-length ride height adjustment, pillow-ball mounts, adjustable camber plates on front/rear in many kits — aiming to balance daily comfort with track-level control

If available, other brands you listed (H&R, Öhlins, Holley) may also exhibit, but as of current searches I couldn’t find confirmed coilover-product announcements for SEMA 2025 from them. If you want, I can try to track those down.

Technology & Trend Highlights for Coilovers in 2025 SEMA

  • Multi-stage damping adjustability: separate settings for high-speed bumps / low-speed body movement.
  • Hybrid systems: coilover + air conversion options (like what Air Lift is offering with its coilover-to-air upgrade path).
  • Lightweight & durable materials: anodized aluminum bodies, corrosion resistant coatings; lighter springs and mounts.
  • Digital / Smart integration: sensors, app control, data logging.
  • Modularity & tuning: easy adjustability (droop, bump, rebound), height, spring rates.

Exhibition Suggestions

To maximize benefit if you are exhibiting or attending:

  • Plan in advance which exhibitor booths to visit (Air Lift, KW, KONI, etc.).
  • Monitor press releases & pre-SEMA product previews from these brands.
  • Attend the New Products Showcase portion of SEMA – often where many coilover innovations are first revealed.
  • Check legal/regulatory & certification details (especially for international dealers).

Conclusion

SEMA 2025 will be a pivotal platform for the coilover suspension segment. For manufacturers, it’s prime time for product launches and global visibility; for consumers and distributors, it’s where new trends become clear in damping adjustability, material engineering, hybrid solutions, and digital integration. Keeping an eye on confirmed exhibitors like Air Lift Company, KW Automotive North America, and YELLOW SPEED RACING, especially in their coilover lines, will give strong indications of what’s coming in the aftermarket suspension world.
