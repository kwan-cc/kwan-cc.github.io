    EMO MILANO 2027: A Journey to The Centre of Innovation

    Mar 10, 2026

    Event Overview

    EMO MILANO 2027 will be held from 4 to 8 October 2027 at the fieramilano exhibition centre in Milan, Italy. It is established as the world’s leading exhibition dedicated to the metalworking industry. The official website presents the event as “a journey to the centre of innovation,” highlighting practical, factory-ready technologies rather than purely conceptual ideas. For industry professionals, EMO MILANO serves as a global marketplace where machine tool builders, automation providers, digital manufacturing experts, and end-user manufacturers meet to evaluate process capabilities and compare production technologies across sectors. As part of the rotating EMO cycle, the 2027 edition returns to Milan following the German show, reinforcing its status as a key European platform for metalworking decision-makers and suppliers.

    2025 to 2027 Technology Continuity

    EMO Hannover 2025 placed strong emphasis on automation and artificial intelligence as key drivers of production efficiency. Although EMO MILANO 2027 introduces the theme “journey to the centre of innovation,” the technology focus outlined on the official site shows clear continuity rather than a complete shift. Digital manufacturing, AI, automation systems, and smart production technologies remain at the core. This reflects a typical pattern in industrial exhibitions — messaging may evolve, but the underlying technology direction stays consistent. EMO 2027 can therefore be viewed as building upon the 2025 priorities while embedding them within a broader innovation narrative that also encompasses machine tools, additive manufacturing, metrology, and related services.

    Technology Scope and 2027 Themes

    The official EMO MILANO website covers the entire production chain, with particular emphasis on metal cutting and metal forming machine tools, robots and automation systems, additive manufacturing, digital solutions, and artificial intelligence. It also includes tooling, parts and components, metrology, quality control, and services. This comprehensive scope allows visitors to assess not only individual machines but how different technologies integrate into complete manufacturing processes.

    For buyers, this structure is especially valuable because metalworking projects typically involve multiple interdependent technologies. The main categories can be summarized as follows:

    • Metal cutting and metal forming machine tools
    • Robots and automation systems
    • Additive manufacturing
    • Digital manufacturing and process automation, including AI
    • Tooling, parts, components and accessories
    • Metrology, quality control and services

    These categories demonstrate that EMO MILANO 2027 supports full production decision-making rather than isolated equipment purchases.

    Organizers and Market Positioning

    Organized under the auspices of CECIMO and operated by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, EMO MILANO 2027 benefits from strong backing by Europe’s leading manufacturing technology institutions. The fieramilano venue is promoted for its excellent facilities and transport links, an important practical advantage for international exhibitors and visitors. The event positions itself as the world exhibition for metalworking, promising high international participation and a diverse range of suppliers.

    This positioning offers suppliers access to a truly global audience of OEMs and Tier manufacturers with active investment plans, while buyers gain the opportunity to compare European and international technologies side by side in one location.

    Previous Edition Context

    The website notes the return to Milan after six years, placing the 2027 edition firmly within the established EMO rotation. Although specific attendance figures are not highlighted, the site stresses the quality and quantity of business contacts made by exhibitors and the comprehensive technological offering appreciated by visitors. This suggests EMO MILANO functions as a serious business platform where practical procurement discussions take place, consistent with its long-standing reputation in the metalworking community.

    Taiwan Exhibitor Snapshot (EMO Hannover 2025 Reference)

    The following Taiwanese companies appeared on the EMO Hannover 2025 exhibitor platform. Their inclusion here serves as a neutral reference for Taiwan’s presence in the EMO cycle. Participation in 2027 is not confirmed. Each profile is based on the companies’ official websites and focuses on their core capabilities and relevance to metalworking buyers.

    Victor Taichung

    Victor Taichung positions itself as a comprehensive CNC machine tool builder, offering multi-tasking turning centers, 5-axis machining centers, CNC lathes, machining centers, wheel machining solutions, and turnkey systems. The company also emphasizes automated integration and intelligent equipment, indicating a focus on complete manufacturing solutions. Its applications cover semiconductor, new energy, aerospace, medical, and transportation industries. A global service network further supports its export-oriented model and after-sales commitment.

    Falcon Group (CHEVALIER brand)

    Falcon Machine Tools, operating under the CHEVALIER brand, specializes in grinding machines, lathes, and milling machines, with a particularly strong emphasis on grinding technology. Its range includes CNC surface grinders, rotary surface grinders, double-column grinders, vertical grinding centers, as well as machining centers and turning equipment. The brand’s SMART iControl and iSurface systems aim to enhance precision, stability, and ease of operation. Applications span aerospace, semiconductor, automotive, mold manufacturing, and automation — sectors that demand high surface quality and dimensional accuracy.

    JINN FA Machine

    JINN FA MACHINE INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD., founded in 1976, is a specialist manufacturer of CNC precision metal lathes and machining centers. Its product lineup includes Swiss-type CNC lathes, fixed headstock CNC lathes, turret CNC lathes, and compact economical models. With over 50 patents and more than 25,000 machines produced, the company focuses on high-quality, versatile solutions for precision parts. Key application sectors include aerospace, electronics, medical, and automotive components. JINN FA maintains a global presence in over 40 countries with service support in multiple regions.

    Shun Chuan Machinery

    Shun Chuan Precision Machinery Co., Ltd., established in 1994, specializes in both conventional and CNC metalworking lathes. The range covers gear-head, variable-speed and clutch-type manual lathes, as well as flat-bed and slant-bed CNC lathes. Backed by strong R&D and manufacturing experience, the company is recognized for product reliability and has exported machines to more than 70 countries worldwide. Shun Chuan offers a complete spectrum from traditional to modern CNC lathes, appealing to buyers seeking precision, stability, and cost-effectiveness.

    SYIC (Shin-Yain)

    SYIC manufactures precision tool holders, angle heads, boring heads, and collets, supporting a wide range of taper standards including BT, CAT, HSK, PSC, VDI, and BMT. The products are designed for machining centers, mill-turn machines, and workpiece clamping systems. In addition to tool holders, SYIC offers measurement tools, cutting tools, and accessories, enabling support across multiple stages of the machining process. This makes SYIC a key supplier for standardized and customized tool-holding solutions compatible with global machine tool platforms.

    Earth-Chain Enterprise

    Earth-Chain Enterprise is a leading Taiwanese manufacturer of CNC magnetic workholding systems, best known for its electro-permanent magnetic chucks. The product portfolio includes precision grinding and machining center series, quick mold change systems, permanent magnetic clamping blocks, and battery-type electro-permanent lifting magnets. With over 30 patents, ISO 9001 and CE certifications, the company focuses on innovation, reliability, and energy efficiency. Its solutions are widely used in automated production, precision grinding, vertical/horizontal machining, and plastic injection molding, and are exported to more than 40 countries.

    Buyer and Supplier Value

    For buyers, EMO MILANO 2027 provides a single, efficient venue to evaluate machine tools, automation, digital technologies, tooling, and metrology solutions that directly impact productivity, precision, and cost of ownership. For suppliers, the event’s global reputation and Milan location offer direct access to international decision-makers with active investment plans. Supported by CECIMO and UCIMU, EMO MILANO 2027 remains a credible platform for serious business discussions and long-term relationship building in the metalworking industry.

    • Date: 4–8 October 2027
