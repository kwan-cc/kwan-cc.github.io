WIN EURASIA 2026: Automation And Machine Technologies Fair

Mar 13, 2026

  • Event Name: WIN EURASIA 2026
  • Dates: June 10–13, 2026
  • Venue: Istanbul Expo Center, Yeşilköy, Istanbul, Turkey
  • Organizer: Hannover Fairs Turkey Fuarcılık A.Ş.
  • Official Website: www.win-eurasia.com / platform.win-eurasia.com

Overview

WIN EURASIA is a major comprehensive industrial trade fair in the Eurasia region, covering automation, machine manufacturing, welding technologies, energy management, logistics, and digital factory solutions. The 32nd edition will be held under the theme "Driven by Automation", highlighting the role of automation technologies in improving efficiency, flexibility, and sustainability in modern manufacturing processes.

The exhibition is expected to occupy 6 halls with a total display area of approximately 55,000 m². It serves as a practical platform for suppliers, manufacturers, system integrators, and buyers to showcase equipment, exchange technical information, and facilitate business matching.

Main Technology and Product Categories

The fair will feature the following key areas:

  • Electric and Electronic Transmission, Distribution, and Energy Automation
  • Industrial and Factory Automation Technologies
  • Robotic Automation and Digital Factory Solutions
  • Welding and Robotic Welding Technologies
  • Machine Automation and Technologies
  • COMVAC (Compressed Air and Vacuum Technologies)
  • Joining, Cutting, and Surface Treatment Technologies
  • Logistics, Intralogistics, and Supply Chain Solutions
  • Energy, Electrotechnology, and Fluid Power Systems

Dedicated zones will include 5G application areas, Hydrogen & Fuel Cells, Welding Application Zones, and an Industrial Artificial Intelligence section, showcasing the integration of IoT, 5G, AI, and other digital technologies in manufacturing.

Previous Edition (2025) Key Figures

The most recent edition provided the following metrics:

  • 702 exhibitors from 15 countries
  • 55,000 m² exhibition space across 6 halls
  • 40,243 visitors from 86 countries
  • Over 200 VIP buyers from 25 countries
  • 1,221 arranged B2B meetings through the matchmaking platform
  • 74 conference and panel sessions with 200 speakers

These figures indicate the event's scale and its role as a venue for international business engagement and knowledge exchange.

Taiwan Participation

Taiwan has been represented at WIN EURASIA in recent editions, with companies from the country participating as international exhibitors. Past editions (including 2025) included Taiwanese firms among the international participants from countries such as Germany, China, Japan, Korea, and others. Examples of Taiwanese companies that have exhibited or are listed in connection with the fair include:

  • ASHUN Fluid Power Co., Ltd. (specializing in hydraulic components and AS9100-certified fluid power solutions)
  • Sesame Motor Corp. (offering planetary gearboxes and related motion control products)

As of early 2026, the full exhibitor list for the 2026 edition is not yet fully published or searchable on the official platform. Interested parties can check the official exhibitor directory on platform.win-eurasia.com for updates, search by company or country, or contact the organizer for the latest information on Taiwanese exhibitors.

Taiwan's involvement aligns with its strong presence in global manufacturing and automation sectors, particularly in components, machinery, and fluid power technologies. No dedicated Taiwan national pavilion has been announced for WIN EURASIA 2026 based on available information, unlike some other international fairs where Taiwan organizes grouped pavilions.

Expected Focus in 2026

The 2026 edition will continue to emphasize automation-led innovations across various manufacturing sectors. Visitors can expect demonstrations of advanced systems in robotics, digital factory integration, energy-efficient solutions, and AI-enhanced processes. The fair also includes conference programs, technical seminars, and structured matchmaking opportunities for industry professionals.

WIN EURASIA 2026 remains a significant regional platform for exploring current trends and technologies in industrial automation and related fields.
