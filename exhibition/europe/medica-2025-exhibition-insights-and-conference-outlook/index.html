MEDICA 2025 Exhibition Insights And Conference Outlook

Mar 26, 2026

MEDICA 2025 is scheduled for November 17–20, 2025, at Messe Düsseldorf in Germany. As the world’s largest trade fair for medical technology and healthcare, it has earned its title as the “World Forum for Medicine,” continuing to attract thousands of global professionals and innovators each year.

Known Exhibition Details

  • Dates & Venue: November 17–20, 2025, at Messe Düsseldorf, Germany.
  • Scale & Reach: In recent editions, MEDICA hosted approximately 5,100 to 5,800 exhibitors from over 70 countries and welcomed around 80,000 trade visitors.
  • Global Participation: A substantial share of exhibitors and visitors come from outside Germany, underlining its international scope.
  • Parallel Event: MEDICA 2025 will be held alongside COMPAMED, a specialized exhibition focusing on medtech suppliers and components.

Confirmed Exhibitors & Notable Participants

Several companies are already listed in the official MEDICA 2025 Exhibitor Index, indicating their confirmed participation. Examples include:

  • E&O Laboratories Ltd.[
  • Diwei Industrial Co., Ltd.
  • [ICST CORPORATION](What's New 2025 | ICST CORPORATION) (Saitama, Japan)

These names represent a sample of confirmed exhibitors; the full directory includes many more across all segments of the medical technology industry.

Exhibition Domains

MEDICA 2025 will cover a broad and dynamic range of sectors, including:

  1. Medical technology & electromedicine
  2. Laboratory technology & diagnostics
  3. Medical imaging
  4. Health IT & telemedicine (digital health)
  5. Mobile health (mHealth)
  6. Physiotherapy & orthopaedic technology
  7. Medical consumables & disposables
  8. Emerging themes such as AI, robotics, sustainability, and connected health solutions

These domains are often arranged as thematic “experience areas” to facilitate focused exploration by attendees.

Seminars, Forums & Conferences

MEDICA’s supporting program is equally extensive, designed to facilitate dialogue, innovation, and networking:

  • MEDICA Innovation Forum (incorporating the Health IT Forum): Central platform for digital health topics like electronic patient records, data security, and healthcare IT.
  • Start-up Competitions: Annual presentations such as "Top 12 Medical Start-ups" and "Health Techpreneurs" spotlight emerging solutions in digitally networked healthcare.
  • Additional Formats: Includes expert talks, panel discussions, product demos, award ceremonies, and political/business dialogues.
  • Digital Visitor Tools: MEDICA’s website offers pre-event resources such as exhibitor listings, matchmaking tools, and event planning aids to help visitors maximize engagement.

Conclusion

MEDICA 2025 is shaping up to be an impressive convergence of global medical expertise and innovation. With thousands of exhibitors already confirmed—including companies like E&O Laboratories, E-CARE MEDICAL, Medscope Biotech, ICS Medical Devices, and Anika Therapeutics—the event promises a panoramic showcase across imaging, diagnostics, health IT, and more. The fusion of a vast exhibition, the MEDICA Innovation Forum, start-up contests, and enriched digital tools ensures that attendees will have deep access to knowledge, connections, and market trends.

Whether you're a healthcare veteran, a start-up innovator, or a medical tech supplier, MEDICA remains an essential stage for visibility, collaboration, and shaping the future of healthcare.
