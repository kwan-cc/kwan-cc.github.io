Anuga 2025 – Where Sustainability Meets The Future of Global Food Trends

Mar 27, 2026

From October 4 to 8, 2025, the Cologne Exhibition in Germany will host Anuga 2025, the world’s leading food and beverage trade fair. With the guiding theme of “Sustainable Growth,” this international event will highlight innovation, sustainability, and global food trends.

A Global Hub for the Food Industry

Anuga has long established itself as the most influential international meeting point for the food and beverage sector. The last edition welcomed nearly 7,900 exhibitors from 118 countries and about 140,000 visitors from 200 nations. In 2025, attendance is expected to remain equally strong or even greater, as the fair continues to serve as the central hub for networking, trade, and innovation. ([2025 Anuga Exhibitor](Exhibitor search on the Anuga 2025))

Ten Specialized Trade Shows Under One Roof

Anuga’s unique format brings together ten trade shows, each highlighting a core area of the global food and beverage industry. This structure provides both breadth and depth, enabling professionals to explore interconnected sectors while focusing on their specific interests.

  1. Anuga Alternatives – A new addition in 2025, spotlighting alternative proteins and future-oriented food innovations.
  2. Anuga Bread and Bakery – Covering bread, baked goods, and snack products, with both traditional and modern specialties.
  3. Anuga Chilled and Fresh Food – Featuring fresh convenience foods, ready meals, and delicatessen products tailored to evolving consumer lifestyles.
  4. Anuga Dairy – Focused on milk, cheese, and other dairy innovations, alongside plant-based alternatives gaining market share.
  5. Anuga Drinks – Presenting beverages from non-alcoholic refreshment drinks to functional beverages and alcoholic products.
  6. Anuga Fine Food – The largest segment, dedicated to gourmet and delicatessen products, sauces, spices, and premium packaged goods.
  7. Anuga Frozen Food – Showcasing the expanding frozen products sector, emphasizing preservation, convenience, and innovation.
  8. Anuga Hot Beverages – Concentrating on tea, coffee, and cocoa products, along with preparation and service trends.
  9. Anuga Meat – One of the most significant sectors, covering red and white meat, processed products, and hybrid innovations.
  10. Anuga Organic – Dedicated to certified organic food and beverages, reflecting demand for sustainable and natural products.

Together, these ten shows provide a comprehensive cross-section of the food industry, ensuring visitors access to both established categories and emerging markets.

The Central Theme: Sustainable Growth

Sustainability is not just a trend but a necessity, and “Sustainable Growth” has been chosen as the overarching theme of Anuga 2025. The focus will be on issues that are critical to the future of food production, such as circular food systems, supply chain transparency, climate-friendly production, and resource efficiency. Strategic collaborations with organizations like EIT Food and UNIDO highlight the fair’s commitment to global partnerships that push for real change in how food is produced and consumed.

A Redesigned Hall Layout

The new hall plan introduced for Anuga 2025 has been carefully designed to improve orientation and create stronger synergies between sectors. For example, Anuga Alternatives and Anuga Fine Food will be located together in Hall 1, with Fine Food also extending into Hall 5.2. Meanwhile, Anuga Organic has been placed closer to Alternatives to stimulate cross-category interaction, making it easier for visitors to navigate between conventional products and sustainable innovations. This thoughtful arrangement enhances networking opportunities and ensures that industry players can discover trends and partnerships across related segments.

Spotlight on Alternative Proteins

The launch of Anuga Alternatives as a dedicated trade show is one of the most anticipated features of 2025. This section will showcase the fast-growing market of alternative proteins, including plant-based, algae-derived, mushroom-based, and lab-cultivated products. In addition to product displays, the program will feature webinars, workshops, and expert discussions, offering insights into one of the industry’s most dynamic areas.

A Robust Events and Conference Program

Beyond the exhibition halls, Anuga 2025 offers a strong platform for knowledge exchange. The first Anuga Halal Forum will address the growing halal food market, while the Tomato News Conference will provide a global perspective on the tomato industry. On October 7, the second International Frozen Food Conference, led by IFFN and GCCA, will highlight innovation in frozen products. These events ensure that Anuga remains a space not only for trade but also for thought leadership.

Strategic Partnerships for Innovation

Anuga’s strength also lies in its ability to connect business with innovation. Partnerships with organizations like BALPro will drive discussions on alternative proteins, while the new ANUGA HORIZON at the Conflex venue will serve as a forum for investment, innovation, and sustainable business models. Together, these initiatives position Anuga as both a marketplace and a catalyst for transformation in the food industry.

Conclusion

Anuga 2025 in Cologne is more than just a trade fair. It is a forward-looking platform where sustainability and innovation converge. With ten interlinked trade shows, a redesigned hall concept, the introduction of Anuga Alternatives, and a strong emphasis on Sustainable Growth, the event promises to remain one of the most influential gatherings for food and beverage professionals worldwide.
