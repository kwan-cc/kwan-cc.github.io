What is LogiMAT Stuttgart?

LogiMAT Stuttgart is one of the world's most influential trade fairs dedicated to intralogistics solutions, warehouse technologies, and supply chain optimization. Held annually at the Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre in Germany, the exhibition gathers leading global manufacturers, logistics experts, and industrial decision-makers to explore the latest innovations that improve warehouse productivity, automation, and operational efficiency.

The 2026 LogiMAT Stuttgart exhibition will take place from March 24–26, 2026, bringing together thousands of logistics professionals, procurement managers, system integrators, and warehouse operators from around the world. The event serves as a key platform for discovering next-generation material handling equipment, intelligent warehouse systems, and practical logistics solutions designed to address the evolving challenges of modern distribution and manufacturing environments.

For companies seeking to enhance warehouse efficiency, optimize space utilization, and improve operational safety, LogiMAT is one of the most important industry events of the year.

Solving Today’s Warehouse Challenges

Warehouse managers and logistics procurement teams face several ongoing challenges:

  • Increasing warehouse density and limited storage space
  • Rising labor costs and the need for higher operational efficiency
  • Safety requirements for intensive material handling environments
  • Demand for durable equipment capable of operating continuously

Tailift focuses on solving these challenges through engineering-driven forklift design, emphasizing:

  • Reliability in demanding industrial environments
  • Ergonomic and safety-focused operator design
  • Efficient energy usage and durable components
  • Flexible product configurations for diverse applications

These principles allow businesses to reduce downtime, improve productivity, and maximize warehouse space utilization.

Forklift Solutions on Display at LogiMAT 2026

At our booth, visitors will discover a wide range of material handling equipment designed to meet different warehouse and industrial requirements.

Electric Counterbalance Forklifts

Tailift will showcase advanced electric counterbalance forklift trucks, engineered for quiet operation, energy efficiency, and low maintenance requirements. These forklifts are ideal for indoor logistics operations such as manufacturing facilities, distribution centers, and retail warehouses.

Key benefits include:

  • Zero emissions for indoor environments
  • Lower operating and maintenance costs
  • Smooth and precise handling performance

IC Counterbalance Forklifts for Heavy-Duty Applications

For outdoor logistics operations and heavy-duty industrial environments, Tailift offers robust IC counterbalance trucks and Internal combustion cushion tire forklifts designed for durability and power.

These models are widely used in:

  • Construction materials logistics
  • Ports and container yards
  • Manufacturing plants
  • Heavy industrial operations

Their rugged design ensures consistent performance even in demanding conditions.

VNA Forklifts for High-Density Warehousing

As warehouse storage density continues to increase, many logistics operations are turning to Very Narrow Aisle (VNA) forklifts to maximize storage capacity.

Tailift’s VNA articulated forklifts are designed to:

  • Operate efficiently in narrow aisles
  • Increase warehouse storage capacity
  • Improve picking and pallet handling efficiency

These machines are ideal for high-bay warehouses, logistics hubs, and distribution centers where space optimization is critical.

Electric Stackers and Warehouse Handling Equipment

For pallet movement, stacking, and short-distance transport tasks, Tailift also offers versatile electric pallet stackers and material handling equipment.

These compact machines are widely used in:

  • Retail warehouses
  • Food and beverage logistics
  • Light manufacturing facilities
  • E-commerce fulfillment centers

They provide a practical and cost-effective solution for daily warehouse operations.

OEM Forklift Manufacturing Capabilities

In addition to our standard forklift lineup, Tailift also supports OEM and customized forklift manufacturing for global partners and equipment distributors.

With decades of manufacturing experience, Tailift offers:

  • Flexible product customization
  • Strong engineering and R&D support
  • Reliable supply chain integration
  • Long-term partnership opportunities for global distributors

This capability allows businesses to develop tailored material handling solutions that align with their specific market requirements.
