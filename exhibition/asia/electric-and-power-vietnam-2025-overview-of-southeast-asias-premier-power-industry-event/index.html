Electric and Power Vietnam 2025 Overview of Southeast Asia's Premier Power Industry Event

As Vietnam continues its path toward industrial modernization and energy diversification, Electric and Power Vietnam 2025 emerges as one of the region’s key trade fairs connecting international energy technology providers with Southeast Asia’s fast-growing power sector. This article offers a neutral and verified look at the upcoming exhibition, including confirmed features, themes, and participants, based only on reliable and publicly available sources.

Event Overview

Organized by Informa Markets and officially supported by the Vietnam Ministry of Industry and Trade and Vietnam Electricity Corporation (EVN), Electric and Power Vietnam 2025 will take place from November 12 to 14, 2025 at the Saigon Exhibition and Convention Center (SECC) in Ho Chi Minh City.

The event will cover more than 8,800 square meters of exhibition space, welcoming over 210 exhibitors and more than 5,000 professional visitors. It serves as a regional hub for energy professionals working across power generation, transmission, electrical infrastructure, and clean energy development.

Source: PCBShop.org

Key Themes and Industry Coverage

The exhibition’s technical scope covers:

  • Electric power generation and distribution systems

  • Grid modernization and automation

  • Renewable energy and hybrid integration

  • Capacitor and energy storage technologies

  • Smart control systems and industrial electrification

This broad range makes it a vital event for stakeholders such as utilities, EPC contractors, system integrators, OEMs, and policymakers.

Source: TradeIndia – EPV 2025

Confirmed Exhibitor

As of this writing, Yuhchang Electric Co., Ltd. is the only exhibitor with a publicly confirmed announcement of participation in Electric and Power Vietnam 2025.

  • Yuhchang Electric Co., Ltd.
    A trusted Taiwanese capacitor manufacturer with over 50 years of experience, Yuhchang has officially announced its participation in the 2025 edition of the exhibition. The company will present its full range of advanced capacitor solutions, including:

    • High Voltage and Low Voltage Power Capacitors

    • Power Electronic Capacitors

    • Induction Furnace Capacitors

    These products are widely used in applications such as energy storage, reactive power compensation, harmonic filtering, and high-frequency industrial heating. Yuhchang’s presence reflects the show’s emphasis on technical depth and application-ready solutions in the evolving energy landscape.

As the official exhibitor list is expected to expand closer to the event date, stakeholders should monitor the event website) or Informa’s official directory for the most accurate updates.

Why This Event Matters

1. Strategic Energy Positioning
Vietnam is experiencing sustained electricity demand growth, driven by urbanization, industrialization, and renewable energy goals. Events like EPV provide a space to exchange expertise and accelerate access to critical technologies.

2. Regional Market Expansion
The show connects international suppliers with Vietnamese buyers, offering foreign companies a gateway into one of Southeast Asia’s most dynamic electricity markets.

3. Technology-Driven Solutions
The convergence of players in power electronics, smart grids, and renewable integration highlights the region’s evolving approach to energy resiliency and efficiency.
