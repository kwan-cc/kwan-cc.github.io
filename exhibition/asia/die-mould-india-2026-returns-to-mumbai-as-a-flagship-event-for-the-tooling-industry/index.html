Die & Mould India 2026 Returns to Mumbai as A Flagship Event for The Tooling Industry

Mar 11, 2026

The 14th Die & Mould India International Exhibition will take place April 21–24, 2026 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. Organized by the Tool & Gauge Manufacturers Association of India (TAGMA‑India), the event is positioned as India’s largest exhibition dedicated to the die and mould fraternity and a key platform for the tooling ecosystem.

A Trade Fair Built Around Tooling’s Role in Manufacturing

Often called the “mother industry,” tooling underpins nearly every manufacturing sector, from automotive and consumer appliances to electronics and packaging. Die & Mould India is designed to bring the full value chain together—tool rooms, die and mould makers, machine tool suppliers, materials providers, and software specialists—creating a single marketplace for business development and technology discovery.

The exhibition is held biennially and has built a consistent presence since it was conceptualized in 1998. For many Indian and international suppliers, it serves as the primary venue to introduce new capabilities, meet OEMs and Tier suppliers, and engage with decision‑makers responsible for production lines and new program launches.

2026 Scope: What Visitors Can Expect

Based on the published exhibition scope, Die & Mould India 2026 will cover a broad range of tooling-related technologies and applications, including:

  • Tool rooms: die casting dies, rubber moulds, plastic injection moulds, jigs/fixtures, gauges, and sheet‑metal dies
  • Machine tools and machining centers: CNC, EDM, and precision machining systems
  • Moulding and forming: injection moulding machines and sheet‑metal presses
  • Design and engineering software: CAD/CAM/CAE and simulation tools
  • Materials and components: steel suppliers, standard parts, mould bases, electrodes, graphite, and consumables
  • Process support: heat treatment, surface treatment, inspection and quality systems (CMM), and welding

This combination makes the show relevant not only to toolmakers but also to OEM buyers and manufacturing managers looking to evaluate suppliers across a full production chain.

Previous Edition Highlights (DMI 2024)

The 13th edition (February 14–17, 2024) provides a clear benchmark for scale and market traction. Official figures report:

  • 300+ exhibitors from 15+ countries
  • 25,000 sq. m of exhibition space
  • 38,000+ visitors from 15+ sectors
  • 35+ products launched during the show
  • 15+ OEMs participating in a B2B Pavilion
  • A Korean Pavilion featuring around 10 companies
  • Delegations from 25+ companies

These results underscore the event’s role as a high‑intent sourcing platform rather than a general‑interest expo.

Industry Focus and Buyer Profiles

The exhibition positions itself as a cross‑sector tooling event, with the most relevant visitor segments coming from:

  • Automotive OEMs and auto ancillary suppliers
  • Heavy engineering and industrial manufacturing
  • Electrical and electronics manufacturing
  • Consumer appliances and packaging
  • Plastic processing and precision components

For buyers, the show is a practical venue to evaluate tooling partners, compare manufacturing technologies, and align supplier capabilities with upcoming production needs.

Exhibitor Landscape: What’s Likely in 2026

A detailed exhibitor list for 2026 has not been published yet. However, based on the event’s scope and recent editions, the exhibitor mix typically includes:

  • Tool and die manufacturers with advanced tooling capabilities
  • Machine tool and EDM/CNC suppliers
  • Moulding and forming equipment providers
  • CAD/CAM/CAE software vendors
  • Inspection, metrology, and quality control specialists
  • Materials, consumables, and surface treatment suppliers

The presence of a B2B Pavilion and national pavilions in prior editions signals continued emphasis on structured buyer‑seller engagement and international participation.

Why Die & Mould India Matters in 2026

For manufacturers, the event arrives at a time when production localization, tooling quality, and throughput efficiency are key competitive factors. For toolmakers, it offers a rare opportunity to meet OEMs and Tier suppliers in a concentrated setting, validate demand, and demonstrate process capability. For overseas companies, Die & Mould India remains a practical entry point into the Indian tooling and manufacturing market.

Event Details

  • Event: 14th Die & Mould India International Exhibition
  • Dates: April 21–24, 2026
  • Venue: Bombay Exhibition Centre, Mumbai, India
  • Organizer: TAGMA‑India (Tool & Gauge Manufacturers Association)
