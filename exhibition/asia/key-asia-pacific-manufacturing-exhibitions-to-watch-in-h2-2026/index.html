Key Asia-Pacific Manufacturing Exhibitions to Watch in H2 2026

Jul 17, 2026

For OEM/ODM buyers and industrial procurement teams sourcing from Asia-Pacific manufacturing networks, the region's H2 2026 exhibition calendar is concentrated in a roughly six-week window across September and October, spanning mainland China and Hong Kong.

Tube China and Wire China: The Region's Metalworking Anchor

Tube China and Wire China, both running September 21 to 24 at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre, are among the region's leading exhibitions for tube, pipe, and wire processing technology, drawing manufacturers and buyers from across the metalworking and material processing supply chain. For buyers sourcing tube, pipe, or wire processing equipment and components, this co-located pairing offers the most concentrated supplier comparison opportunity in the region for this category.

Hong Kong's Electronics and Lighting Fairs

Hong Kong hosts two significant HKTDC-organized fairs in the same window. The Hong Kong Electronics Fair (Autumn Edition) runs October 13 to 16 at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, while the Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (Autumn Edition) follows October 27 to 30 at the same venue. Both fairs draw substantial international buyer attendance and are well established stops for electronics, component, and lighting sourcing across the Asia-Pacific region.

Valve and Hardware Shows Rounding Out the Region

The China International Hardware Show runs September 28 to 30 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai, bringing together hardware manufacturers, importers, and retailers from across Asia and Europe. The Zhejiang International Valve Industry Chain Fair follows October 9 to 11 in Wenzhou, offering a more specialized venue for valve and pipeline component sourcing focused on the Chinese domestic supply base.

The full Asia-Pacific H2 2026 calendar is summarized below.

Show Dates Location Focus
Tube China / Wire China Sep 21-24 Shanghai, China Tube, pipe, and wire processing
China International Hardware Show Sep 28-30 Shanghai, China Hardware manufacturing and retail
Zhejiang International Valve Industry Chain Fair Oct 9-11 Wenzhou, China Valve and pipeline components
Hong Kong Electronics Fair (Autumn) Oct 13-16 Hong Kong Electronics and components
Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (Autumn) Oct 27-30 Hong Kong Lighting sourcing

How OEM/ODM Buyers Should Prioritize Asia-Pacific Show Attendance

With five shows spread across roughly six weeks in three cities, prioritization comes down to matching the show to a specific sourcing category rather than attending broadly: a buyer focused on tube and wire processing gets far more value from Tube China and Wire China than from an electronics-focused fair, and vice versa.

For buyers already sourcing from Taiwan or broader Asia-Pacific manufacturing networks, the September-October window offers an efficient way to combine supplier facility visits with trade show attendance in a single trip, since the Shanghai and Hong Kong shows fall within about six weeks of each other. Booking travel and supplier meetings early matters given how tightly the shows are clustered, particularly for buyers planning to combine multiple shows with factory visits across the region.

FAQ

Q: Are there major industrial exhibitions in Asia during H2 2026 besides China-based shows?

A: Yes. Hong Kong hosts two significant HKTDC fairs in the same window, the Hong Kong Electronics Fair (Autumn Edition) from October 13 to 16 and the Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (Autumn Edition) from October 27 to 30, both at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. These draw substantial international buyer attendance separate from the mainland China show calendar.

Q: Can one trip realistically cover both the Shanghai shows and the Hong Kong fairs?

A: Yes, with careful planning. Tube China and Wire China run September 21 to 24 in Shanghai, followed by the China International Hardware Show in the same city from September 28 to 30. The Hong Kong Electronics Fair runs October 13 to 16, roughly two weeks after the Hardware Show. Buyers with sourcing needs spanning metalworking, hardware, and electronics can combine the two cities into a single extended trip, while adding the Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (October 27 to 30) would extend the itinerary into late October.

Conclusion

Asia-Pacific's H2 2026 exhibition calendar gives OEM/ODM buyers a concentrated set of opportunities to evaluate suppliers across metalworking, electronics, valve, and hardware categories, spread across Shanghai, Wenzhou, and Hong Kong within roughly six weeks. Prioritizing shows by specific sourcing category, and planning travel around the tightly packed September-October window, is the most efficient way for buyers sourcing from the region to make use of this year's calendar.
