Deciding between HDBaseT 3.0 and 2.0 depends primarily on your requirement for uncompressed color depth and USB data rates. HDBaseT 3.0 is the definitive choice for environments requiring uncompressed 4K@60Hz (4:4:4) video and high-speed USB 2.0 over a single 100m Cat6A cable. While HDBaseT 2.0 remains a cost-effective solution for digital signage and standard presentations where slight chroma subsampling (4:2:0) or compression is acceptable, it cannot match the 18Gbps bandwidth of the 3.0 specification. For future-proofing high-stakes environments—such as medical imaging, high-end command centers, or pro-gaming—the upgrade to HDBaseT 3.0 is not just recommended; it is essential to eliminate latency and image artifacts.
