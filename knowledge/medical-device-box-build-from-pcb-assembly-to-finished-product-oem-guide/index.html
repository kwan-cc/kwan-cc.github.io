Medical Device Box Build: From PCB Assembly to Finished Product (OEM Guide)

Apr 21, 2026

Introduction

As medical devices become more complex and regulated, OEMs are shifting from sourcing individual components to working with integrated manufacturing partners.

Instead of managing multiple vendors for PCB assembly, wiring, enclosure, and testing, companies increasingly rely on providers like Funet Technologies that offer complete system integration.

This approach is known as medical device box build—a critical capability for modern medical product development.

What Is Medical Device Box Build?

Medical device box build refers to the full assembly of a medical product after PCB assembly (PCBA), transforming electronic boards into a finished, functional system.

What Does Box Build Include?

  • Enclosure (housing) integration
  • Cable and wire harness assembly
  • Display and user interface installation
  • Power system integration
  • Final assembly and inspection

In simple terms, box build converts a PCB into a ready-to-use medical device.

Medical Device Box Build Process (Step-by-Step)

1. PCB Assembly (PCBA)

  • SMT and through-hole processes
  • Precision soldering and inspection

2. Sub-Assembly Integration

  • Cable harness and connectors
  • Sensor and module integration

3. Enclosure & Mechanical Assembly

  • Housing installation
  • Thermal management
  • EMI shielding

4. System Integration

  • Firmware programming
  • Interface calibration

5. Testing & Validation

  • Functional testing
  • Safety testing
  • Burn-in

Medical devices must comply with standards defined by the International Electrotechnical Commission, such as IEC 60601.

6. Packaging & Traceability

  • Clean packaging
  • Serial labeling and tracking

Why Medical Box Build Is Critical for OEMs

Reduced Supply Chain Complexity

Working with one supplier simplifies procurement and coordination.

Improved Quality Control

End-to-end responsibility ensures consistency.

Faster Time-to-Market

Integrated workflows eliminate delays between stages.

Compliance & Traceability

Manufacturers must meet requirements such as ISO 13485.

Challenges in Medical Device Box Build

Regulatory Compliance

Strict documentation and certification requirements

High Reliability Expectations

Zero-defect manufacturing is essential

Low-Volume, High-Mix Production

Customization increases production complexity

System Integration Risks

Electrical and mechanical alignment must be precise

Box Build vs Traditional PCBA

Aspect Traditional PCBA Medical Box Build
Scope PCB only Full system
Responsibility Partial End-to-end
Testing Board-level System-level
Compliance Lower High

Applications of Medical Device Box Build

  • Patient monitoring systems
  • Infusion pumps
  • Dental equipment
  • Home healthcare devices

How to Choose a Medical Box Build OEM Partner

This is one of the most critical decisions in medical product development.

Key Evaluation Criteria

  • ISO 13485 certification
  • Integrated PCBA + box build capability
  • Advanced testing and validation
  • Full traceability system
  • Engineering and design support

Manufacturers such as Funet Technologies provide complete EMS solutions, helping OEMs reduce complexity while ensuring compliance and product reliability.

Conclusion

Medical device box build has become a core capability in modern medical electronics manufacturing.

By integrating PCB assembly, mechanical design, system integration, and testing, OEMs can achieve:

  • Higher product quality
  • Faster time-to-market
  • Better regulatory compliance

Choosing the right partner can significantly impact the success of your medical device project.
