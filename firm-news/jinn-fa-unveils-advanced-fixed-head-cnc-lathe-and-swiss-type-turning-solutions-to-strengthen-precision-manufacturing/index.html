JINN FA Unveils Advanced Fixed Head CNC Lathe And Swiss-Type Turning Solutions to Strengthen Precision Manufacturing

Mar 27, 2026

Precision component manufacturers today face several major challenges: shorter delivery cycles, rising labor costs, increasing product complexity, and the need for highly consistent mass production. For procurement managers and production engineers in industries such as medical devices, electronics, automotive components, and precision instruments, selecting the right fixed head CNC lathe or Swiss-type turning solution is critical to maintaining both productivity and quality.

To address these manufacturing demands, JINN FA MACHINE INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD., a Taiwan-based specialist in precision CNC lathes, has introduced two new machines designed for modern production environments:

  • JCL-52TSII-A Fixed Head CNC Lathe (dual spindle, dual turret series)
  • JSL-12RBY Swiss-Type CNC Lathe

These machines demonstrate JINN FA’s long-standing expertise in machine structure design, machining precision, and integrated manufacturing technology.

Why Modern Manufacturers Need More Advanced Fixed Head CNC Lathe Solutions

Manufacturers across global supply chains are under pressure to improve efficiency without sacrificing accuracy. Traditional turning systems often struggle with:

  • Multiple setups for complex parts
  • Long cycle times for high-volume production
  • Inconsistent precision between operations
  • Limited flexibility for mixed part production

A well-designed fixed head CNC lathe with multi-axis capability and dual processing systems can significantly reduce these issues by enabling simultaneous machining, multi-operation integration, and improved production stability.

JINN FA’s newly released machines directly target these pain points.

JCL-52TSII-A Fixed Head CNC Lathe: Dual-Spindle Efficiency for High-Volume Production

The JCL-52TSII-A fixed head CNC lathe is designed for manufacturers that require both productivity and structural rigidity. This high-end model integrates dual spindles, dual turrets, and Y-axis machining capability, allowing complex parts to be processed in a single setup.

Key Capabilities

  • Maximum turning diameter: 52 mm
  • Main and sub spindle speed: up to 5,000 rpm
  • Dual turret system with powered tools
  • Y-axis machining for complex geometry
  • Machine weight: approximately 5 tons for superior rigidity

The machine’s dual-system architecture enables independent cutting operations, allowing both sides of a workpiece (A/B surfaces) to be completed in a single cycle. This significantly reduces part handling and machining time, making the machine ideal for high-efficiency batch production.

Production Advantages

Feature Manufacturing Benefit
Dual spindle design Complete front/back machining in one cycle
Dual turret with powered tooling Multi-process machining without repositioning
High structural rigidity Stable precision in continuous production
Y-axis capability Supports complex part geometries

For industries that require high repeatability—such as automotive components, connectors, and industrial fittings—this fixed head CNC lathe platform helps achieve both speed and accuracy at scale.

JSL-12RBY Swiss-Type CNC Lathe: Designed for Ultra-Precision Small Parts

While the JCL series focuses on productivity, the JSL-12RBY Swiss-type CNC lathe targets the rapidly growing market for micro and ultra-precision components.

With a maximum machining diameter of 12 mm, the machine is optimized for small and complex parts often used in high-value industries.

Typical Applications

  • Medical implants and artificial joint components
  • Precision instrument internal parts
  • Electronic connectors
  • Complex miniature mechanical components

A key feature of the JSL-12RBY is its convertible guide bushing system, allowing manufacturers to switch between Swiss-type sliding head machining and conventional turning modes depending on the application.

Combined with a versatile tooling system, the machine can perform multiple machining processes in a single platform, improving overall production efficiency by approximately 40%.

Integrated Machining Is the Future of Precision Manufacturing

According to JINN FA General Manager Huang Chin-Fa, the future of CNC turning technology lies in continuous innovation and process integration.

Manufacturing trends shaping the industry include:

  • AI-assisted machining optimization
  • Automation and smart factory integration
  • Multi-tasking machine tools
  • Higher efficiency and safer production environments

By investing heavily in R&D and expanding its machine lineup, JINN FA aims to provide manufacturers with flexible solutions—from high-efficiency fixed head CNC lathe platforms to ultra-precision Swiss-type turning systems.

As precision manufacturing enters a new era driven by automation and integrated machining, JINN FA continues to advance Taiwan’s machine tool innovation while supporting global manufacturers in achieving higher efficiency, better precision, and smarter production.
