New Bellus NB® 23 Granted Japanese Patent for Muscle Mass And Metabolic Health – A New Benchmark for Probiotic Raw Material Suppliers

Mar 19, 2026

A New Benchmark for Probiotic Raw Material Suppliers in Sports Nutrition and Healthy Aging

In the rapidly evolving global health supplement industry, brands and procurement teams are under increasing pressure to identify scientifically validated, differentiated probiotic ingredients that can support high-value product innovation.

NEW BELLUS ENTERPRISES CO., LTD. recently announced that its proprietary probiotic strain New Bellus NB® 23 (Lactobacillus paracasei NB23) has been granted a Japanese invention patent (Patent No. 7778416) by the Japan Patent Office.

The patented technology—titled “Lactobacillus paracasei NB23 Strain and Its Use for Increasing Muscle Mass and Anti-Metabolic Syndrome”—highlights the growing importance of clinically validated probiotic solutions in the fields of sports nutrition, metabolic health, and healthy aging.

For product developers, supplement brands, and procurement professionals seeking a reliable probiotic raw material supplier, this milestone offers a new layer of scientific credibility, regulatory protection, and product differentiation.

Why Patented Probiotic Ingredients Matter for Supplement Brands

In today’s competitive nutraceutical market, generic probiotic strains are no longer sufficient for brands aiming to stand out. Buyers increasingly look for patent-backed probiotic ingredients with clinical validation that can support clear functional claims.

Key procurement challenges in probiotic ingredient sourcing include:

  • Lack of unique strain differentiation
  • Limited clinical evidence for specific health outcomes
  • Difficulty in supporting regulatory and marketing claims
  • High competition in the sports nutrition and healthy aging segments

Patented strains such as New Bellus NB® 23 help solve these challenges by offering exclusive functional positioning supported by scientific evidence.

What Makes New Bellus NB® 23 Unique?

Clinically Validated Probiotic for Muscle and Metabolic Health

New Bellus NB® 23 was discovered through the company’s proprietary New Bellus Functional Strain Screening Platform, which focuses on identifying probiotic strains with targeted physiological benefits.

Clinical research has demonstrated the strain’s potential in three critical areas:

  • Muscle health support
  • Metabolic regulation
  • Physiological balance for healthy aging

Unlike conventional probiotic ingredients that focus primarily on digestive health, NB® 23 expands the functional scope of probiotics into metabolic and muscle-related applications.

This positions the strain as a strategic ingredient for brands developing next-generation sports nutrition, active aging, and wellness supplements.

Addressing the Growing Demand for Muscle and Metabolic Wellness

Global consumers are increasingly focused on preventive health management, particularly in areas such as:

  • Muscle preservation

This shift is driving demand for functional probiotic strains with targeted physiological benefits, rather than traditional general-purpose probiotics.

New Bellus NB® 23 addresses these needs by supporting:

  • support for daily mobility in aging populations

For supplement developers and procurement teams, these functional attributes can translate into clear product positioning and strong market narratives.
