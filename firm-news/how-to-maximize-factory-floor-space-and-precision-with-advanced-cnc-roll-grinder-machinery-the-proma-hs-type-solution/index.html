How to Maximize Factory Floor Space And Precision with Advanced CNC Roll Grinder Machinery: The PROMA HS-Type Solution

Mar 04, 2026

In today's highly competitive industrial landscape, factory owners and procurement professionals face a constant dilemma: how to achieve ultra-high precision machining while minimizing equipment footprint and controlling capital expenditures. Traditional grinding machines often consume massive amounts of factory space, require frequent maintenance due to rapid component wear, and lack the software flexibility needed for complex modern manufacturing.

Addressing these exact pain points, PROMA MACHINERY CO.,LTD. has introduced a game-changing solution. Their HS-Type grinding and polishing compound machine—a top-tier cnc roll grinder machinery—has become a beacon of Taiwanese engineering, offering a cost-to-performance ratio that surpasses established European and Japanese international brands.

Solving Key Procurement Pain Points: The PROMA Advantage

For B2B buyers evaluating new capital equipment, the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and Return on Investment (ROI) are critical metrics. The PROMA HS-Type cnc roll grinder machinery is engineered to directly resolve common manufacturing bottlenecks through several innovative features:

1. Maximizing Factory Space Efficiency (Up to 40% Reduction)

Factory floor space is a premium asset. The HS-Type is designed with a moving wheelhead architecture that significantly reduces the machine's footprint. Compared to similar models on the market, it saves up to 40% in floor space, directly increasing the utilization efficiency and revenue-generating potential per square meter of your facility.

2. Unmatched Spindle Rigidity for Heavy-Duty and Delicate Tasks

The grinding wheel spindle is crafted from high-grade Nickel-Chromium-Molybdenum (Ni-Cr-Mo) alloy steel. Undergoing rigorous tempering, heat treatment, dynamic balance calibration, and precision grinding, the spindle guarantees stable, vibration-free cutting performance. With a maximum distance of 2 meters between centers, it is perfectly scaled for diverse industrial demands.

3. Advanced Hybrid Lubrication for Zero-Friction Operation

Both the spindle and the worktable utilize an advanced hydrodynamic and hydrostatic hybrid circulating lubrication system. This ensures continuous, ultra-smooth movement, drastically reducing the friction and wear of core components. For facility managers, this means longer machine lifespans, reduced downtime, and lower maintenance costs.

4. Software Flexibility for Complex Profiles

Modern machining requires versatility. The HS-Type comes equipped with highly expandable software capabilities, offering operators a variety of curve programming options, including Sine, Cosine, CVC, and custom empirical values.

Proven Applications Across High-Tech Industries

With dozens of units successfully deployed over the past seven years, this star machine has proven its reliability and versatility across multiple demanding sectors. Key applications include:

  • Plastic Injection Molding: High-precision grinding of tie bars (interfering columns).
  • Optics and Display Technology: Mirror finishing and grinding/polishing of thin-film rollers, such as brightness enhancement films.
  • Energy Sector: Heavy-duty grinding for oil drilling equipment bits.
