Deploying Edge AI in Industrial Automation: The MediaTek Genio 360 EVK

Mar 23, 2026

Modern industrial facilities require high computing power directly on the factory floor. Relying on cloud infrastructure for machine vision or real-time equipment monitoring introduces latency and data security risks. Edge AI solves these operational bottlenecks by processing data locally.

Finding the right hardware balance between thermal efficiency, processing capability, and long-term availability is a primary challenge for system integrators. The MediaTek Genio 360 Evaluation Kit (EVK) provides a concrete hardware foundation for these industrial requirements. InnoComm has secured the exclusive global development and sales rights for this platform, ensuring reliable hardware supply and technical support for enterprise deployments.

Hardware Specifications Built for the Factory Floor

Industrial applications demand specific hardware tolerances. The Genio 360 and its high-performance variant, the Genio 360P, are built on a 6nm process technology. This specification delivers a high performance-to-power ratio, which is critical for fanless industrial PC (IPC) designs.

The platform integrates a hexa-core or octa-core CPU architecture alongside an 8th-generation MediaTek NPU. The NPU delivers between 5.1 and 7.4 TOPS of AI inference performance. This dedicated AI processor allows production lines to run complex local models without overloading the main CPU. Furthermore, the industrial-grade version of the Genio 360 chipset supports an operating junction temperature range of negative 40 to 105 degrees Celsius, making it suitable for harsh manufacturing environments.

Core Industrial Use Cases for the Genio 360 Platform

The computing architecture of the Genio 360 EVK directly aligns with three major industrial automation trends.

Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)

Quality control requires processing high-resolution image streams with zero delay. The Genio 360 supports multiple camera inputs and features a hardware video encoder capable of handling FHD90 streams. The 5.1 TOPS NPU processes these visual inputs locally to identify microscopic product defects on fast-moving conveyor belts.

Predictive Maintenance Systems

Equipment failure causes costly production downtime. By connecting vibration and acoustic sensors to the comprehensive I/O interfaces of the Genio 360, factories can continuously monitor motor and bearing health. The edge AI processor analyzes this sensor data to predict mechanical failures before they occur.

Advanced Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI)

Factory operators need clear data visualization. The EVK supports single 4K60 or dual FHD60 display outputs. This graphic capability allows integrators to build detailed local dashboards and control panels for complex machinery.

Transitioning from Evaluation to Mass Production

Procuring an evaluation kit is the first phase of hardware deployment. Translating a successful bench test into a deployable industrial product requires significant engineering resources. Many industrial projects face delays during carrier board design and operating system porting.

As the exclusive system integrator for the MediaTek Genio 360 EVK, InnoComm provides end-to-end ODM services to solve these integration challenges. The company's engineering team assists industrial clients with carrier board customization tailored to specific factory form factors. InnoComm handles the strict thermal design requirements for enclosed industrial spaces and provides complete OS porting services for Yocto Linux or Android.

By utilizing InnoComm's ODM services, an enterprise's internal software engineering team can focus entirely on training machine vision algorithms and developing application software, rather than troubleshooting hardware compatibility.
