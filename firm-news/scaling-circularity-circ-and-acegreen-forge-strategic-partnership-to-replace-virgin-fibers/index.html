Scaling Circularity - CIRC And Acegreen Forge Strategic Partnership to Replace Virgin Fibers

Sep 03, 2025

A Five-Year Purchase Agreement Sets the Stage for Sustainable Fiber Supply

CIRC, a U.S.-based innovator in textile-to-textile recycling, and Taiwan’s Acegreen Eco-Material Technology (a subsidiary of Acelon Chemicals & Fiber Corporation) have formalized a five-year purchase agreement in April 2025 to supply recycled pulp and polyester from CIRC’s first commercial-scale facility.

The partnership builds on initial collaboration that began in June 2023, when Acegreen first started using CIRC’s recycled cotton pulp for fiber production trials. Acegreen will utilize the recycled pulp to produce pre-specified lyocell fibers, while Acelon commits to integrating CIRC’s recycled polyester into its polyester filament production.

What’s Behind the Agreement?

  • Long-Term Commitment
    Acegreen has pledged to purchase recycled cotton pulp and recycled polyester produced by CIRC’s first commercial-scale facility over the next five years.

  • End Products
    The recycled cotton pulp will be converted into pre-specified lyocell fibers, while Acegreen’s parent company, Acelon, will integrate CIRC’s recycled polyester into polyester filament production

  • Leadership Voices
    Peter Majeranowski, CEO of CIRC, noted that Acegreen was among the first partners to integrate CIRC’s recycled materials into filament production—essential in demonstrating real-world scalability.

    Roger Chou, CEO of Acegreen, emphasized that the collaboration underscores a shared mission: securing supply while advancing resource efficiency and waste reduction across the textile value chain.

  • Verified Sustainability Credentials
    Acegreen holds the Dark Green Shirt ranking in Canopy’s Hot Button report for both 2023 and 2024, a badge of forest-friendly Man-Made Cellulosic Fiber (MMCF) practices.

Why This Matters for the Industry

  • Addressing Supply Constraints: As demand for recycled materials continues to outstrip supply, this long-term agreement ensures a stable channel of circular raw material for both CIRC and downstream fiber producers.

  • Driving Systemic Change: Roger Chou, Acegreen’s CEO, highlights that the collaboration not only secures supply but also reinforces a collective mission to reduce waste and enhance resource efficiency in textiles.

  • A Blueprint for Others: Such partnerships exemplify how innovators and manufacturers can coalesce to bring sustainable materials into mainstream production—paving the way for broader industry transformation.

Looking Ahead

The collaboration between CIRC and Acegreen highlights a broader shift within the textile industry toward textile-to-textile recycling solutions. Similar initiatives, such as Inditex’s multi-year agreement with Ambercycle, underscore a growing commitment to scaling recycled fiber production.

By securing a reliable supply of high-quality recycled pulp and polyester, the CIRC–Acegreen partnership serves as a concrete example of how circular solutions can be implemented at commercial scale. As the industry continues to embrace circularity, such collaborations demonstrate that sustainability and high-performance materials can coexist, setting a benchmark for other companies to adopt similar approaches and contribute to a more resource-efficient and environmentally conscious supply chain.
