AUTOGRIP MACHINERY’s Fully Sealed 3HS-08 Power Chuck Wins TMBA Silver Energy-Saving Label

Sep 06, 2025

Changhua, Taiwan – August 2025 – AUTOGRIP MACHINERY CO., LTD., a global leader in power chucks and workholding solutions, proudly announces that its newly developed 3HS-08 hollow power chuck has been awarded the Silver Energy-Saving Label by the Taiwan Machine Tool & Accessory Builders’ Association (TMBA). This prestigious recognition underscores AUTOGRIP’s commitment to sustainable manufacturing and precision engineering.

The 3HS-08 is a 3-jaw, fully sealed, low-maintenance power chuck designed specifically for high-precision machining and demanding, automated environments. Its enclosed construction not only prevents the ingress of chips and coolant—significantly reducing maintenance—but also ensures stable clamping force, which is crucial for maintaining machining accuracy over time.

“As manufacturers pursue smarter and greener production, the 3HS-08 reflects AUTOGRIP’s mission to deliver solutions that combine high efficiency, precision, and environmental responsibility,” said the company in a statement.


AUTOGRIP awarded the TMBA Silver Energy-Saving Label for its 3HS-08 fully sealed power chuck.

Key Features of the 3HS-08:

  • ▶️ Fully Sealed Design – Prevents contamination and reduces maintenance frequency
  • ▶️ Stable Clamping Performance – Ensures high precision even in high-speed and automated operations
  • ▶️ Eco-Friendly Engineering – Aligns with global trends in energy conservation and carbon reduction
  • ▶️ Award-Winning – Recognized by TMBA with the Silver Energy-Saving Label

Ideal for automated production lines and harsh machining conditions, the 3HS-08 not only enhances long-term performance but also helps manufacturers maximize economic efficiency while moving toward greener operations.
